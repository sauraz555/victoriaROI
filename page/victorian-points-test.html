          What changed for Victoria in 2026–27

          On 13 October 2026 the Victorian Government published the weightings of its points test and introduced fees for the first time.

          Published points

          The same seven factors as before, now with published scores out of 950.

          New fees

          $60 + GST for an ROI and $180 + GST for a 190 or 491 nomination application.

          Re-submit your ROI

          ROIs from earlier program years will not carry over. A new ROI is needed when the program opens.

          4 priority industries

          Healthcare, Social assistance, Education and Construction get their own invitation stream.

          2026–27 fees

          FeeExcl. GSTIncl. GST*When charged

          *GST applies if you are in Australia at the time of payment.

          How Victoria selects ROIs

          StreamWho is invited in each round
          Most competitiveThe highest VPT scores across all eligible occupations in the pool.
          Priority industryThe highest VPT scores within each priority industry: Healthcare, Social assistance, Education (Teachers and Early Childhood) and Construction (Professionals and Trades).

          You also need at least 65 points on the Australian Government points test (including nomination points) to be eligible for the visa. The VPT only decides your ranking for Victorian nomination.

          Three changes that catch people out

          Partner points are narrower

          Your partner must qualify for the 10 SkillSelect partner points, be included in your visa application, and have an occupation on Victoria's priority list. Otherwise they score 0 in the VPT, even though they still count for 10 federal points.

          Earnings now carry real weight

          Earnings from current skilled employment in Victoria are worth up to 150 points. Under $80,000, or living outside Victoria, scores 0. You must be able to evidence what you claim, or the nomination can be refused.

          Old ROIs don't carry over

          In 2025–26, earlier ROIs stayed valid. For 2026–27, everyone must lodge a new ROI (and pay the $60 + GST fee) when the program opens.

          What's not confirmed yet

          The points test and fees are confirmed. As at 13 October 2026, Victoria has not yet published:

          • The 2026–27 program opening date.
          • Victoria's 2026–27 nomination allocation. For reference, 2025–26 was 3,400 places, of which 2,700 were for the 190.
          • Full 2026–27 eligibility criteria, including residence, minimum English and offshore 491 rules.

          Victoria's experience table also jumps from "3–5 years" to "6–8 years" without saying how 5 to 6 years is scored. Talk to us if this applies to you.

          Get ready before the program opens

          • Work out your VPT score with the checker above.
          • If your English is Competent or Proficient, consider re-testing. It is the biggest gain most applicants can make.
          • Check whether your occupation is on the 2026–27 priority list.
          • If you claim partner points, check your partner's occupation is on the priority list.
          • If you work in Victoria, calculate your annual earnings and keep evidence ready.
          • Make sure your SkillSelect EOI is accurate and up to date. The VPT draws on it.
          • Budget $66 for the ROI and $198 for the nomination if invited (GST-inclusive, paying from Australia).
          • Lodge a new ROI as soon as the program opens. Old ROIs will not be considered.
